Many of our hypertensive patients are also at high risk of developing other cardiovascular events or may have history of coronary artery disease, cerebrovascular event or diabetes mellitus. For these patients, it is important to consider a hypertensive drug that not only provides 24-hour blood pressure control, but also provides proven cardiovascular protection and even renal benefits. Benefits that can be found in Boehringer Ingelheim’s Telmisartan.1-4
 
Unlike other ARBs, Boehringer Ingelheim’s Telmisartan** has been proven to provide Cardiovascular Protection (relative risk reduction in CV Death, non-fatal MI, non-fatal stroke and hospitalization of congestive heart failure) similar to the gold standard, Ramipril, in high CV risk patients. In addition, Telmisartan also reduces proteinuria in hypertensive patients with diabetic nephropathy.1-4
