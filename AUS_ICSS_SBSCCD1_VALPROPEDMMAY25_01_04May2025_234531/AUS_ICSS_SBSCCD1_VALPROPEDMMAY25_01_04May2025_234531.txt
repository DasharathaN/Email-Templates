2. Extend your cash flow
When your statement arrives, you’ll have a choice of repayment options. By making higher repayments over a shorter term, you'll have more available credit to use when needed. You can pay off the entire closing balance on, or before, the statement due date, in which case you’ll avoid paying interest. Or, you can choose to pay your closing balance over time by paying at least the minimum monthly payment amount. This option will mean you will be paying interest. For more information see Credit Card Repayments at americanexpress.com/au/credit-cards/about-credit-cards/repayments 

3. Manage your limit
All American Express Credit Cards have a maximum Credit limit. Your highest limit will depend on your financial circumstances and the Credit Card you choose and will be subject to our approval criteria. You should ensure that you nominate a Credit Card limit that’s in line with your current financial circumstances. For more information see Credit Card Limits at americanexpress.com/au/credit-cards/about-credit-cards/limits/.
